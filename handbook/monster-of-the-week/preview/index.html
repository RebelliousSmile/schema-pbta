Livret

The Lightkeeper

You keep a light that has not been needed for shipping in forty years, and every night you keep it anyway. Something in the channel counts on the dark, and you are the reason it does not get it.

Répartition de départ

Charm +0, Cool +2, Sharp +1, Tough +1, Weird -1 — or swap Cool and Weird.

Why did you take the light?

  • It was my parent's, and they did not finish the job.
  • The county pays badly and asks nothing.
  • Something asked me not to.

What is in the logbook you never show anyone?

  • Every night the beam caught something that was not a boat.
  • Names, in a hand that is not mine.
  • Nothing. That is the problem.

Read the Water

2d6 + Weird

You have spent enough nights watching the channel to know when it is lying to you. Standing water, wet footprints, a puddle that ripples with no wind: they all answer, if you ask in the dark.

Déclencheur

When you question still water about what has passed through it, roll +Weird.

  • lightkeeper
  • ritual
10+

Ask two of: what came through here, where did it go, what does it want, who else has asked me this? On a 12, the water shows you rather than telling you.

7-9

Ask one of those questions. Whatever you asked about learns that you asked.

Miss

The water answers something you did not ask, and it is about you.

basic

Stand the Watch

2d6 + Cool

Someone has to stay awake while the others sleep, and the thing outside knows it.

Déclencheur

When you keep watch through the night in a place you know is not safe, roll +Cool.

10+

Nothing reaches the others, and you notice one thing about how it moves.

7-9

Nothing reaches the others, but the watch costs you: mark harm 1 or lose your next chance to rest.

Miss

You wake to find the watch already over, and something inside with you.

class

Sightline

The beam of your light is the one thing in the county nothing can stand in front of and stay hidden.

Déclencheur

When you sweep your light across a place, name what you are looking for; the Keeper tells you whether it is there.

class

Fog Sense

You always know how long you have before the fog closes the road. Not to the minute, but close enough to decide.
