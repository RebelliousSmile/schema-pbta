Livret

The Ember

Something burned in your house and you were the only one who walked out warm. You are the last heat of a thing that ended. People treat you as a memorial, and you are trying very hard to be a person instead.

Choose one spread, or move one point from any stat to another.

What did the fire take?

  • A building everyone in the city can name.
  • A person only you remember.
  • The version of you that had plans.

Who pulled you out?

  • A stranger who never gave a name.
  • Someone on your team, who will not talk about it.
  • Nobody. You walked.

Moves du livret

playbook

Borrowed Nerve

You are braver on someone else's behalf than you have ever been on your own. This is not courage you own. It is lent, and the lender can call it back.

When you act on a promise you made to someone who holds Influence over you, take +1 forward.

basic

Hold the Line

Standing your ground is a claim about who you are, and the city will test it. When you hold the line you are not attacking, and you are not hiding. You are telling everyone watching where the edge of the fight is.

When you plant yourself between danger and someone who cannot take the hit, roll + Danger.

Miss

The line breaks somewhere you were not looking. Mark a condition, and the GM says who got through.

Mixed success

You hold, but only by giving something up. Choose one: you take the blow meant for them and mark a condition, or the line holds and something behind you is wrecked.

Full success

Nothing gets past you. Take Influence over anyone who watched you do it, and clear a condition if this is the person you swore to protect.

playbook

Bank the Coals

You can put yourself out, briefly, and nobody likes what is underneath. Going cold is not rest. It is a debt taken against the next time you need to be warm.

When you deliberately go cold to pass for ordinary, you may hold your heat until the end of the scene.

  • Take another move from your playbook.
  • Take a move from another playbook.
  • Unlock your Moment of Truth.
  • Change your hero name and your look.
